The GLB was built from selected FJ mesh segments in the official
isa_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip archive. BodyParts3D represents anatomical concepts
using a 3D model of an adult human male. NeuroLab groups selected source
segments into display regions, rotates and scales the model for the viewer,
recomputes mesh normals, and applies the region colors. Region coverage is
documented in src/workspaces/Brain/brainVisualizationData.ts.

To regenerate the GLB with Node.js 22+ and unzip installed:
node --experimental-strip-types scripts/build-brain-atlas.mjs /path/to/isa_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip

The cortical lobe groups contain selected source gyri rather than complete
lobe masks. The model does not provide a separate prefrontal-cortex segment.
